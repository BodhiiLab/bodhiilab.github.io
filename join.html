Curious about why models work?

BODHI Lab is building a small, collaborative group of PhD students, postdocs and undergraduates who enjoy both machine learning and biology. You do not need to be an expert in both; you need to care about getting the explanation right.

PhD students

If you are applying to a PhD program at Stony Brook, mention BODHI Lab in your application and send a short email introducing yourself.

Postdocs

Lead a direction

Send a CV, two representative papers, and a paragraph on a question you would like to pursue with the lab.

Undergraduates

Stony Brook students with programming experience and curiosity about biology are welcome to reach out about research projects.

Send a CV and a short note about one question you would like to answer.
